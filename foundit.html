Features

Everything you need to find & recover

AI Image Matching
Smart visual similarity scoring automatically matches your lost items with found reports using advanced AI.
Location-Based Discovery
Browse items on an interactive map filtered by a 10km radius around your current location.
Secure In-App Chat
Communicate directly with finders or owners through a private, end-to-end secured chat system.
Real-Time Notifications
Instant alerts when someone reports a match, submits a claim, or your item status changes.
Admin Verification
Trained admins review all claims and reports, ensuring safe and legitimate item recoveries.
Cross-Platform
